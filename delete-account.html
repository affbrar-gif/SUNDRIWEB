Delete Your Account

Your data, your choice
You can request deletion of your Sundri account and all data associated with it at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, what gets removed, what may be retained, and how long the process takes.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — Email request Available now

Send a deletion request to the address below. Please include the phone number associated with your Sundri account so we can identify and remove your data.

Email a deletion request

Option 2 — In-app Coming soon

A self-service "Delete my account" button will be added to the Account screen in a future app update. Until then, please use the email option above.

When we process your request, the following are permanently removed from our systems:

What may be retained

Some information may be retained after deletion in line with Google Play and Apple App Store policy:

  • Anonymized aggregate analytics — counts, trends, and patterns that cannot be linked back to you as an individual. These help us understand how the app is used in general.
  • Data required by law — if a legal or regulatory obligation requires us to retain specific records (e.g. fraud investigation, security incident logs), we retain only the minimum required, only for as long as required.

If you'd prefer that your contribution to anonymized analytics also be removed, please mention this in your deletion email and we'll honour it.

How long it takes

Account and data deletion requests are processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive an email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Account-only vs full deletion

In your deletion email, you can specify either of the following:

  • Delete everything — account, all profile data, saves, swipes, in-app activity, and your contribution to anonymized aggregate analytics.
  • Delete account only — remove your phone, name, and other identifiable data; allow your contribution to anonymized aggregate analytics to remain.

If you don't specify, we default to deleting everything.
